About Fleur

Professional Website Development in Medan for SMEs & Businesses

Fleur is a professional website development service based in Medan, here to help SMEs and business owners build a convincing online presence.

As a Medan-based web agency, we truly understand the challenges local businesses face — from limited budgets to the need for fast, ready-to-sell results. We believe a website is not just about good looks, but about results. That's why every website we build is designed to be easily found, easily understood by potential customers, and most importantly: able to drive conversions, not just views.

Why Many SMEs in Medan Trust Fleur

As an SME web service, we know every business has different needs — that's why we never offer a "one size fits all" solution. From home-based businesses, online stores, to service businesses, we help determine the most suitable type of website, complete with transparent cost estimates from the start.

Although based in Medan, our services reach SMEs and businesses throughout Indonesia. Whether you're just starting an online business or want to upgrade an outdated website, the Fleur team is ready to help from free consultation until your website is truly ready to use.

"A website is not just about good looks, but about results: driving conversions, not just views."

Our Commitment

We don't just sell affordable website development services, but also take responsibility: ensuring the websites we build actually work for your business — fast to access, pleasant to look at, and easy to manage yourself going forward.
